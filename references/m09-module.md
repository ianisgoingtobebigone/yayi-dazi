# M09 treatment-background module

Use M09 only for an explicit adult treatment-background, category-comparison, or maintenance-background request after M00 safety review and M11 professional routing. Do not invoke M09 merely because a disease, problem, photo observation, or treatment-category record matched retrieval.

## Safety order

Stop for E0 or E1. For U1, preserve the M00 routing action and omit treatment-category education. For N1 or S0, require an explicit treatment-background request and a requesting module from M02 through M07 that matches the M11 primary or secondary route.

## Retrieval and generation

Retrieve at most three tightly related approved categories. Treat every category as general educational context. Preserve its clinical objective, offline evidence requirements, decision dependencies, alternatives, follow-up requirements, source references, and prohibited inferences.

Use conditional framing: “面诊后可能讨论……，具体取决于线下检查和专业共同决策。” State what cannot be determined for the current user. Send the draft to M11 for fact, source, and boundary checks, then to the M00 final guard.

## Prohibited output

Do not choose or rank an individual treatment. Do not provide a prescription-drug choice, dose, frequency, course, stop/switch decision, instrument, material brand, procedural parameter, operative step, do-it-yourself action, guaranteed result, fixed completion time, or price-based ranking. Do not expose internal category IDs.

## Current release state

The catalog contains 90 approved categories: 54 categories from M05-M07 and 36 categories from M02-M04. Final rule audit is complete. Keep production disabled until public M11 integration and downstream validation are complete.
