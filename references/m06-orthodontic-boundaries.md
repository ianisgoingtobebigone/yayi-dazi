# M06 adult orthodontic and appliance boundaries

Use this reference for adult orthodontic, dentofacial-aesthetic, appliance, oral-hygiene, retention, or post-treatment-change requests. It summarizes internally transformed textbook material; do not reproduce textbook images or long passages.

## Adult orthodontic gate

Adult orthodontic education must remain conditional on professional assessment. Before discussing treatment categories, preserve and route these dimensions:

- current dental, periodontal, mucosal, pain, swelling, or mobility concerns;
- missing teeth, restorations, implants, tooth migration, or changed spacing;
- temporomandibular-joint or oral-function concerns;
- general-health, medication, previous-treatment, and current-appliance context;
- the user's health, function, appearance, time, and maintenance priorities;
- the possible need for periodontal, restorative, maxillofacial, joint, or other multidisciplinary evaluation.

Do not infer suitability, prognosis, a required test, extraction, anchorage, appliance type, surgery, duration, or expected facial result from age, goals, text, or ordinary photographs. M00 owns urgency; M11 owns primary and secondary routing. M06 preserves the orthodontic goal when a health-first route is required.

## Ordinary-photo boundary

M06 may request the minimum task-specific photograph and may consume only M08-linked visible facts. M08 may describe, within a technically suitable image:

- alignment, overlap, spacing, midline, lip posture, tooth display, and bounded horizontal, vertical, or transverse relations in that frame;
- visible integrity and position of fixed, removable, clear-aligner, or retention appliances;
- visible color, elevation, surface discontinuity, blood trace, covering, or surface appearance around an appliance.

Do not convert these observations into a malocclusion diagnosis or grade, millimetre measurement, dental-versus-skeletal origin, periodontal diagnosis, enamel-demineralization/caries diagnosis, appliance-failure mechanism, adherence judgment, relapse diagnosis, or individual treatment decision.

## Appliance, hygiene, and retention responses

- Preserve the device type only when supported by the user's record or an unambiguous visible form; otherwise use a generic device description.
- Do not instruct the user to bend, cut, grind, glue, reposition, force-seat, remove a fixed component, alter elastic use, or change the wear schedule.
- For pain, swelling, bleeding, tissue injury, functional change, a detached or damaged component, a markedly unseated device, or a post-treatment positional change, return the facts to M00 and the original treatment team or a qualified orthodontic professional as appropriate.
- General cleaning, use, and retention information must defer to the treating team's individual instructions. A photograph cannot determine plaque indices, periodontal support, whether treatment is progressing as planned, or the required retention period.
